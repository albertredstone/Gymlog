Privacy Policy for GymLog

Last updated: 16/08/2026

GymLog does not collect, store, or share any personal data with third parties. All information you enter in the app — including workout logs, body measurements, and progress photos — is stored locally on your device only.

The app does not require an account, does not connect to any external server, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.
